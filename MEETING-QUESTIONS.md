# Website Sit-Down: Questions for McKelvey Fine Art Services

Bring this to the meeting. The ⭐ questions are the must-haves: we can't launch without those answers. Everything else can come later.

---

## 1. The Basics ⭐
- [ ] ⭐ What is the official business name? Is it exactly "McKelvey Fine Art Services"?
- [ ] ⭐ What phone number and email should customers use?
- [ ] ⭐ Which email should quote requests from the website go to?
- [ ] ⭐ What is the service area? (City? State? Region? Will he travel for big jobs?)
- [ ] What are the business hours, or is it "by appointment"?
- [ ] Is there a physical address to list, or should it stay private?
- [ ] When was the business started?

## 2. Services ⭐
- [ ] ⭐ Which of these does he offer? (Cross out the rest.)
  - Art handling
  - Installation / de-installation (hanging, salon walls, sculpture)
  - Crating & packing
  - Transport / delivery (local, regional, national?)
  - Storage (climate-controlled?)
  - Other: rigging, lighting, condition reports, collection management, framing, art consulting, estate/moving work?
- [ ] ⭐ For each service, how would he describe it in a sentence or two, in his own words?
- [ ] Are there things he does **not** do, so we don't advertise them?
- [ ] Which service does he most want to get more of? (We'll feature it.)
- [ ] Does he have his own vehicle(s)? Are they climate-controlled or air-ride?
- [ ] Does he have his own storage space?

## 3. Customers
- [ ] ⭐ Who are his main customers? (Private collectors, galleries, museums, interior designers, artists, auction houses, businesses, homeowners?)
- [ ] Who does he *want* more of?
- [ ] How do customers usually find him today? (Word of mouth, Instagram, referrals from galleries?)
- [ ] What questions do customers ask him most often? (These become an FAQ section.)

## 4. About Him / Credibility ⭐
- [ ] ⭐ His full name and title (Owner? Founder?)
- [ ] ⭐ His background: how did he get into this work, and where has he worked or trained? (Museums, galleries, other art handling companies?)
- [ ] How many years of experience does he have?
- [ ] ⭐ Is he insured? How should we phrase it? (e.g. "Fully insured")
- [ ] Any certifications, memberships, or notable institutions he's worked with?
- [ ] Is it just him, or is there a team? Should team members be on the site?
- [ ] What makes him different from competitors? What does he do better?
- [ ] Are there 3 words he'd want people to associate with the business? (e.g. careful, discreet, reliable)

## 5. Proof: Clients, Projects & Reviews
- [ ] Any client testimonials or quotes we can use, with permission? Names or initials only?
- [ ] Are there galleries, museums, or clients whose names or logos we can show? (Confirm he has permission.)
- [ ] Any notable projects worth featuring? (Big installs, famous pieces, tricky jobs.)
- [ ] Does he have Google reviews, Yelp, etc. we could link to?

## 6. Photos & Visuals ⭐
- [ ] ⭐ Does he have photos of his work? (Installations, crates, team in action, truck, storage.)
  - Anything with clients' art or homes: **does he have permission to post it?**
- [ ] ⭐ Is there a headshot or portrait for the About page? If not, can we take one?
- [ ] Does he have a logo? If yes, get the original file (ideally .svg, .ai, .pdf, or a large .png).
- [ ] Are there brand colors or fonts already? Business cards we can match?
- [ ] What look does he want? (Show him examples: minimal gallery, dark and luxurious, or warm and local.)
- [ ] Any competitor or other websites he likes or dislikes the look of?

## 7. Quote / Contact Form
- [ ] What info does he need from someone asking for a quote? (Number of pieces, sizes, location, dates, photos?)
- [ ] Should people be able to upload photos of their pieces?
- [ ] How quickly does he usually reply? (We'll say "we'll respond within X days.")
- [ ] Does he want to list prices or starting rates, or keep it "request a quote" only?

## 8. Online Presence & Accounts ⭐
- [ ] ⭐ Does he already own a domain name (e.g. mckelveyfineart.com)? If so, where is it registered, and who has the login?
- [ ] If not, which domain names does he like? (We'll check what's available.)
- [ ] Does he have a business email yet (e.g. name@hisdomain.com), or does he use Gmail?
- [ ] Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or other accounts to link to?
- [ ] Does he have a Google Business Profile? (This helps a lot with local search.)
- [ ] Is there an existing website we're replacing?

## 9. Logistics & Future
- [ ] ⭐ Is there a target launch date?
- [ ] Budget for a domain and hosting? (A simple site can be hosted free, and a domain is ~$12–20/yr.)
- [ ] Who will update the site later: him, or you?
- [ ] Anything he'd want later? (Blog or news, project portfolio/gallery, online booking, Spanish version?)

---

## Leave the meeting with:
- [ ] Logo file(s)
- [ ] 10–20 good photos (with permission to use)
- [ ] Headshot
- [ ] Domain login info (have him log in himself, and don't write passwords down here)
- [ ] 1–3 testimonials
